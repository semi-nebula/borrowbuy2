The borrow loop

Four steps, both sides photographed

Avg. handover 4 min
01 / REQUEST

Pick the dates

Choose a pickup slot. The daily rate and deposit are read straight from the listing — there is no field either side can edit.

02 / HANDOVER

Photograph before

Both students meet at the pickup point. A photo of the item, timestamped to the minute, is required before it changes hands.

03 / USE

Keep it inside campus

The item stays on campus for the loan window. If it must leave, the lender is told in-app before it does.

04 / RETURN

Photograph after

Second timestamped photo. If the two don't match, a damage claim opens with both images side by side.

Why this is different

A marketplace has ratings.
This has a record.

Anyone can collect five-star reviews. On BorrowBuy, every loan leaves three things behind: a return score, an item-care score, and a timestamped photo pair. You read all three before you lend.

Bad behaviour is priced, not just reviewed. One day late is a grace window. Five days late, or a returned item with a new dent, triggers a fee, a review, and a score drop — in that order.
Two major incidents suspend the account. Suspension is for the rest of the term and blocks new requests, because a bad lender is a risk to every owner on the board.
