The five rules this prototype is built on

The rules are short on purpose.

Borrowing breaks down over three things: a stranger who cannot be found again, a price that changes after the deal, and damage nobody can prove. Each rule below closes one of those gaps.

Rule 01

One student ID, one account

Only verified students of this campus can list or borrow. A second account on the same ID is refused at database level, so a bad review cannot be escaped by starting again.

No phone-number-only signups. No "borrow on behalf of a friend". No cross-campus visibility.
Rule 02

The price cannot move

The owner sets a daily rate and a deposit once, at listing. Both are frozen. When a borrower requests, they see a total computed from those numbers — and there is no input on either side to change it.

Late fees and damage costs are added by the system against published rates, never typed in by the owner.
Rule 03

Two photos, two sides, timestamped

Before handover and again at return: two opposite sides of the item. The app stamps date and time to the minute. Live in-app capture is an option; gallery upload is allowed but still stamped.

No timestamp, no loan. The handover button stays disabled until both sides are captured.
Rule 04

Reviews are earned, not posted

A review can only be written after a loan closes. Bad reviews are allowed after a 5-day delay or a damage claim. Reviews cannot be edited or deleted by either student.

Your profile shows return score, item-care score and average delay — read from the photo record, not from what someone typed.
Rule 05

Major incidents suspend the account

Damage above ₹2,000, or any delay of five days or more, is a major incident. Two major incidents in one academic year suspend the account for the rest of the term: no new requests, no new listings, existing loans must still be settled.

Reinstatement is not automatic. The suspended student writes a request to the campus moderation team, and any owner with an open claim has to confirm it was settled first.
Rule 03 & 05 in practice

The penalty ladder

Every step is published in advance and applied by the system. Nobody negotiates a fee at the door.

On timeScore +1

Returned before the agreed slot, condition photos match. Return score ticks up. No fees, deposit refunded within 24 hours.

Up to 1 day lateNo fee

Grace window. A "returned late" note is logged on the profile but no money or score penalty is applied. Owner is notified the moment the slot lapses.

2–4 days late₹50 / day

Late fee starts, capped at ₹300. Return score drops 8 points. The delay is shown on the public profile as a number, not a comment.

5+ days late, or owner unreachable₹150 / day

Owner can leave a public bad review and file a recovery request. Return score drops 20 points. The item is marked "in dispute" until it comes back.

Returned damagedRepair + ₹500

Owner files a damage claim with the before/after photo pair. Repair cost is charged at quote, plus a ₹500 handling fee. Issue score drops in proportion to the damage band.

Two major incidentsSuspension

Two major incidents within one academic year — damage above ₹2,000, or any 5+ day delay — suspend the account for the term. Reviews and a re-instatement request are required to return.

How each score is built

On-time return60%
Returned as issued30%
Owner reviews10%

Scores recompute when a loan closes. They never move because of a message, a report alone, or a manual edit.

What is never allowed

Hacking, scraping, or accessing another student's account
Changing a price after a request is sent
Taking an item off campus without telling the owner
Re-uploading an old photo instead of a fresh one at return
A second account on the same student ID
A stale photo is caught by the metadata mismatch against the return timestamp. If the EXIF time predates the loan window, the return is rejected and the borrower has to re-capture.
