What happens if it is late

Applied automatically
Within 24 hoursno feeGrace

The return slot lapses and the owner is notified. A "returned late" marker is logged on your profile but nothing is charged and your score does not move.

2 – 4 days late₹50 / day−8 score

Capped at ₹300, taken from the deposit. The delay is published on your profile as a number — the average delay, not a comment from the owner.

5+ days, or unreachable₹150 / day−20 score

The owner can leave a public bad review and file a recovery request. The item is flagged "in dispute" and the deposit is held until it is returned.

Two major incidentsAccount suspended

Major means damage over ₹2,000, or any delay of 5+ days. Suspension runs to the end of the term and blocks all new requests and listings.

"No phone call answer" is evidence, not an excuse. If the owner has called twice over two days and the borrower has not replied in-app, that is logged automatically and counted toward the 5-day escalation.
